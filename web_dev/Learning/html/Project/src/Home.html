Welcome to Tamil Nadu

Tamil Nadu, located in southern India, is a state rich in history, culture, and natural beauty. Known for its ancient temples, classical dance forms, and delicious cuisine, it attracts visitors from around the world.

Cultural Heritage

Tamil Nadu is home to one of the oldest living languages, Tamil, and a vibrant cultural heritage that includes Bharatanatyam dance and Carnatic music.


Magnificent Temples

The state is famous for its Dravidian-style temples, such as the Meenakshi Temple in Madurai and the Brihadeeswarar Temple in Thanjavur.

Tamil Nadu Cuisine

Tamil cuisine is known for its spicy flavors and variety, with dishes like dosa, idli, and sambar being popular worldwide.

Quick Facts

  • Capital:Chennai
  • Official Language:Tamil
  • Area:130,058 km2
