semantic tags:
Semantic tags in HTML are elements that clearly describe the meaning and purpose of the content inside them—both to browsers and developers.

They improve:
✅ Accessibility (screen readers)
✅ SEO
✅ Code readability & maintenance

<header>     <!-- Top section of a page or section -->
<nav>        <!-- Navigation links -->
<main>       <!-- Main content (only one per page) -->
<section>   <!-- Thematic grouping of content -->
<article>   <!-- Independent, reusable content -->
<aside>     <!-- Sidebar / related content -->
<footer>    <!-- Bottom section -->
